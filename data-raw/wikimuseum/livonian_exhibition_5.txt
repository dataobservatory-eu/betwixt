<center><big>'''Other Costume Accessories'''</big><br />exhibition in the [[Vikipeedia:GLAM/Traditional Livonian Clothing|Traditional Livonian Clothing]]</center>

This room of the virtual exhibition brings together various other dress accessories that were not highlighted separately earlier.

First, there are metal ornaments—a ring and brooches dating from the 18th–19th centuries. All the brooches shown here are ring brooches with a closed circle, a type that began to be worn in this region as early as the 15th–16th centuries. Smaller brooches were used to fasten the neck opening of a shirt, while larger ones served to secure outer garments, such as shawls. Two of the brooches bear images of clasped hands, traditionally given as betrothal or wedding brooches.

Next, we see a variety of ribbons and woven sashes, all decorated with relatively simple patterns. The narrower ones are ribbons, commonly used for tying and fastening different items. Most often, such colourful ribbons were used to tie stockings so that they would stay in place on the leg. Sashes were originally used to hold clothing together, but over time they increasingly gained symbolic meaning and were used for ritual purposes. One of the sashes is braided; such braided sashes were often worn by men threaded through trousers.
